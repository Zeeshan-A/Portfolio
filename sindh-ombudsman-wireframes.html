Sindh Ombudsman · Wireframes

22 screensWeb · MobileEN · اردو

Structure before style.

Every screen was drawn in greyscale first, so the structure could be settled before any visual design: what each role sees first, where each action lives, and how English and Urdu share one layout.

W.01 Complaint form W.02 Dashboard EN / UR W.03 Lists W.04 Settings W.05 Three roles W.06 Citizen app
W.01

A complaint form in four short sections

  • 01Four sections, in the order people thinkType, who you are, what happened, which office.
  • 02Required fields marked up frontA note at the top explains the asterisk before anyone starts.
  • 03Child-related complaints as their own typeChosen first, so they can be routed separately.
  • 04An affidavit before submitOne checkbox replaces a signed paper declaration.
  • 05Clear all kept apart from SubmitSo nobody wipes a long form by accident.
W.02

One dashboard, mirrored for Urdu

  • 01Six numbers firstTotals, resolved, new today, overdue, escalations and resolution time.
  • 02Districts compared at a glanceResponse time and case volume by district.
  • 03Mirrored, not just translatedThe sidebar, cards and charts flip for right-to-left reading.
W.03

One pattern for every list

  • 01Filters, numbers, then the tableEvery list page reads the same way.
  • 02Actions follow statusPending shows Approve or Reject; Blocked shows Unblock.
  • 03Officers and citizens, one screenTwo tabs instead of two separate modules.
W.04

Settings staff can change themselves

  • 01Lists managed in the portalOffices, departments, categories and resolution rules.
  • 02Reports ready to exportFiltered by date, department and region.
  • 03A login that feels officialThe Ombudsman's own building, front and centre.
W.05

Three roles, one system

Admins, officers and citizens use the same layout and components. What changes is how much each role sees.

  • 01The sidebar shrinks with the role10 modules for admins, 4 for officers, 2 for citizens.
  • 02Escalate from any listOfficers act from the row, without opening the case.
  • 03Citizens see only their casesSubmitted, pending, resolved and response time.
  • 04Email or phone to sign inCitizens don't need an email address to use the portal.

The citizen app, four tabs

On phones the citizen portal becomes an app with a bottom tab bar, so filing, following and settings are always one tap away.
