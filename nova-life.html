Nova Life: recovery support that doesn't stop at the clinic door.

NOVA LIFE helps people recover from addiction, suicidal ideation and related challenges. Its new mobile app comes with portals for therapists and staff. I took it from a broad vision through discovery to a defined MVP and its design.

01 Context

What is NOVA LIFE?

NOVA LIFE is a US health and wellness organization supporting people through addiction, suicidal ideation and related challenges. It runs structured rehabilitation programs and intensive care, with a team of licensed providers and therapists.

They wanted a mobile app to take that support beyond their facilities, so people could keep getting help throughout their recovery, not just during sessions.

I joined as the UI/UX designer alongside a Product Owner. We started with a discovery phase to turn the client's early ideas into a clear MVP, then designed the patient app and the portals behind it.

Patients

People in recovery who need support, content and check-ins between sessions.

Therapists & providers

Licensed staff who follow their patients' progress and stay reachable.

Admins & staff

The team that manages patients, providers and content.

02 Problem

A big vision, and no clear first version.

NOVA LIFE came with a promising idea for an app. Each part made sense on its own, but together it was a wish list, not a plan a team could build.

02 A

The client's starting vision

Four ideas, all still preliminary:

  • Idea 01On-demand contentVideo sessions, motivational and religious material, and therapy resources.
  • Idea 02AI-powered therapistAn empathetic AI companion for support at any hour.
  • Idea 03Periodic check-insWellness check-ins that track progress over time.
  • Idea 04Therapist accessA direct line to licensed providers and therapists.
02 B

Support stopped at the clinic door

Recovery happens every day, but help was tied to sessions and programs. Between visits, people had little to lean on.

02 C

No priorities, no MVP

There was no agreed first release, so there was nothing to estimate, design or build against yet.

02 D

Three users, one system

Patients, therapists and staff each needed something different, and every flow had to connect them.

02 E

A vulnerable audience

For people facing addiction or suicidal thoughts, an app has to feel calm and safe, especially an AI companion.

02 F

What discovery had to answer

  • Q1Today's processWhat happens when a patient comes in, and which programs follow?
  • Q2The first releaseWhich features matter most for the MVP?
  • Q3The journeysHow do patients, therapists and staff move through the product?

04 Patient app

Calm, simple and low effort, from the first screen.

A.01

Voice-based onboarding

Instead of a long intake form, the AI companion asks 31 questions out loud and the patient simply answers. They can take a break at any point, and review every answer before it's saved.

A.02

Secure, quick sign-in

A one-time code to verify, then a 4-digit PIN with an optional hint, and Face ID or Touch ID, so getting back into the app takes a second.

A.03

Home, library and milestones

A.04

Taking the daily pledge

Nova AI, by text or voice

Profile and settings

05 Portals

The team behind the app, in one place.

P.01

Admin portal

Staff sign in to see patient and therapist accounts at a glance, review therapists, and manage the menus, series and videos patients see in the app.

P.02

Super admin

The super admin invites patients by link, email or QR code, and approves the therapists who sign up, each with their license, documents and a clear status.

P.03

Therapist portal

Therapists verify their license to join, invite patients, then follow each one between sessions: days sober, how they use the app, wellness trends, and an AI summary of their chats.

06 Outcomes

Small daily wins that add up to sober days.

Nova Life is in use by more than 100 of NOVA LIFE's own patients, along with their therapists and the admin team.

Patients keep coming back because staying sober feels like progress they can see: a pledge each morning, milestones to unlock, and short sessions for the hard moments. Therapists see that progress between sessions instead of waiting for the next visit.
