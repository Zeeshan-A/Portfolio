How I work

Design thinking, in faster loops.

I follow design thinking: understand people, frame the problem, explore, prototype, test. AI speeds up the making, so I can run more rounds of testing and refining. The research, the decisions and the final design stay with me.

  1. 01
    Empathize

    Understand the people

    Interviews, stakeholder talks and UX audits. I do these myself, because nothing replaces hearing users first-hand.

    Research insights
  2. 02
    Define

    Frame the right problem

    A clear problem statement, flows and success criteria. When building is fast, choosing the right problem matters most.

    Problem statement
  3. 03
    Ideate

    Explore more, faster

    Figma Agent and Claude Design help me try many directions in the time one used to take. I choose what fits.

    Flows + journeys
  4. 04
    Prototype

    Build fast, refine with intent

    AI gets me to a working prototype quickly. I take it from there: layout, consistency and alignment with the design system.

    Prototypes
  5. 05
    Test & Loop

    Test, learn, go again

    Usability tests show what to fix, and I loop back while it’s still cheap. Then UAT, specs and design QA in Jira until it ships.

    Dev-ready handoff
