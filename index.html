03 How I work

Five steps of design thinking, one journey from people to product.

From discovery to design QA, working directly with CEOs, CTOs, product owners, analysts, developers and QA. AI speeds up the making, so more time goes to real users.

  1. 01
    DISCOVER

    Understand people first

    Interviews, stakeholder workshops and UX audits, so I know who I’m designing for before I draw a screen.

    Research insights
  2. 02
    Define

    Agree on the real problem

    Findings become a clear problem, priorities and an MVP scope that the client, developers and QA all sign off on.

    User journeys & flows
  3. 03
    Ideate

    Explore more, faster

    User journeys, flows and wireframes, with Claude Design, Figma AI and Figma Make to try more directions early.

    WireframesScreen components
  4. 04
    Prototype

    Make it real

    High-fidelity screens on a shared design system: tokens, bilingual components and every breakpoint.

    Clickable prototypes
  5. 05
    Test

    Let users decide

    Usability tests and UAT with real users, then design QA in Jira until it ships.

    Usability feedback

04 Things I believe

Most products don't fail on features. They fail when people give up halfway.

B.01

A long form should feel short.

Section it, save drafts, pre-fill what you already know, and ask only what's needed right now.

B.02

Users are the final reviewers.

I design for what people actually do. NATJAN's app got an ad-hoc inspection flow because Area Managers needed one on site, not because it was on a feature list.

B.03

Every language and screen, from day one.

Urdu RTL, tablet and phone go into the system at the start, not as a port at the end.
