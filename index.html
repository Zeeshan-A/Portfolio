03 Things I believe

Most products don't fail on features. They fail when people give up halfway.

B.01

A long form should feel short.

Section it, save drafts, pre-fill what you already know, and ask only what's needed right now.

B.02

Users are the final reviewers.

I test before I polish. NATJAN's navigation was rebuilt because Area Managers struggled with it, not because I didn't like it.

B.03

Every language and screen, from day one.

Urdu RTL, tablet and phone go into the system at the start, not as a port at the end.

04 How I work

AI speeds up the making, so more time goes to real users.

From discovery to design QA, working directly with CEOs, CTOs, product owners, analysts, developers and QA in Jira.

H.01

Research in the open

Interviews, stakeholder workshops, UX audits and competitor benchmarks, shared as I go.

H.02

Explore faster with AI

Claude Design, Figma AI and Figma Make for quicker synthesis, more directions and earlier prototypes.

H.03

Test with real people

Moderated usability tests and UAT, 35+ participants so far, before anything is final.

H.04

Systems, handoff, QA

Tokens, bilingual component libraries, Dev Mode specs, then design QA until it ships.
