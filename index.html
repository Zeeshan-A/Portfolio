How I work

AI explores. I decide, and finish it by hand.

For the past year, AI has been part of my process, from research to quick prototypes. It gets me to more ideas, faster. The final pass is always mine.

  1. 01
    Research

    Understand what matters

    Research and quick UX audits, with AI to synthesise findings. I decide what actually matters to users.

    Research insights
  2. 02
    Explore

    Explore more, faster

    Figma Agent and Claude Design help me try visual ideas and quick flows and user journeys early. I pick the direction that fits the problem.

    Flows + User Journeys
  3. 03
    Iterate

    Iterate with precise prompts

    AI turns feedback into new versions in minutes. Precise prompts steer it to the result I want.

    Prototypes
  4. 04
    Craft

    Polish and final touch

    The final pass is mine: fixing layout issues, inconsistencies, and invented patterns that don’t exist in the design system.

    Polished Artifacts
  5. 05
    Test & Handoff

    Test it, then hand it off

    Usability tests and UAT, then dev-ready specs and design QA in Jira until it ships.

    Dev-ready handoff
